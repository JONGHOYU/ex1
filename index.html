TRANSIT & LOGISTICS

How to Get Around Andong Like a Pro

Avoid getting stranded with our clear instructions on trains, city buses, and taxi services.

From Seoul (KTX-Eum)

Take the KTX-Eum high-speed train from Cheongnyangni Station (Seoul) directly to Andong Station.

  • Duration: Approx. 2 hours
  • Cost: ~25,100 KRW (Standard seat)
  • Booking: Via Korail English App ("Korail Talk") or website
  • Frequency: Roughly every 1 to 2 hours

Key City Bus Routes

Buses depart from the bus stops directly in front of Andong KTX Station.

  • Bus #210 (Direct to Hahoe): Every 30-40 min, stops right at Hahoe Ticket Booth. (Fare: 1,500 KRW with T-Money)
  • Bus #112 or #113: Andong Station ➔ Downtown Old Market (Jjimdak Alley).
  • Bus #311 or #312: Downtown ➔ Woryeonggyo Wooden Bridge.
  • Pro Tip: Install Naver Map or KakaoMap in English for real-time bus arrivals. Google Maps transit data is limited in Korea.

Taxis & Kakao T

Taxis are convenient and affordable for groups or night visits to Woryeonggyo Bridge.

  • Base Fare: Starts at 4,000 KRW
  • Ride-Hailing: Download Kakao T (English interface available, accepts foreign credit cards).
  • Taxi Card Generator: Use our Show Taxi Driver button below to show the exact Korean destination address on your phone screen!

LOCAL PROTOCOL & TIPS

International Traveler Survival Guide

Essential practical knowledge to make your journey through Andong smooth, respectful, and memorable.

Hahoe Village is not an artificial theme park—real families (descendants of the Ryu clan) have lived here for generations. Please remember:

  • Do not enter private courtyards if the outer gate (Daemun) is closed.
  • Keep noise levels down in the early morning and after sunset.
  • Always ask permission before taking close-up portraits of residents.

In traditional Korean architecture (Hanok, pavilions, tearooms, and temple prayer halls):

  • Always remove your shoes before stepping onto wooden wooden porches (Maru) or indoor ondol floors. Slippers or bare feet/socks only.
  • Wear comfortable, easy-to-slip-on walking shoes because you will be taking them off frequently.
  • At Buddhist temples like Bongjeongsa, do not step directly on the threshold beam of wooden doorways; step over it.

The UNESCO-inscribed Hahoe Mask Dance Drama is performed regularly at the Mask Dance Theater near the entrance of Hahoe Village:

  • Schedule: Usually Wednesday to Sunday at 2:00 PM (March - December). Arrive 20 minutes early for good seats.
  • Admission: Included free with your Hahoe Village ticket!
  • Language: Subtitles in English, Japanese, and Chinese are displayed on the electronic boards beside the stage.

Due to national spatial data regulations, Google Maps has limited walking directions and transit tracking in South Korea. We strongly suggest downloading:

  • Naver Map: Fully available in English; accurate bus timing, walking navigation, and opening hours.
  • Papago: The most natural Korean-English voice and photo translator (by Naver).
  • Kakao T: To call taxis without needing to speak Korean to the driver.

Traditional Andong Soju is not the green-bottle soju found in convenience stores; it is a meticulously distilled artisan liquor made from pure rice, yeast, and pure underground water. It has an alcohol content of 40% to 45% ABV. Sip it slowly from small porcelain cups to savor its smooth, floral aroma!
